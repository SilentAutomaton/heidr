# mute — reading

[HEID//R](../../../README.md) · [Documentation](../README.md) · [Modules](../README.md#modules) · [Русский](../../ru/modules/mute.md)

## What it does

Nothing. It yields no lines at all.

## Where the data comes from

Nowhere. The material is already on the screen; the silence is the reading.

## How it is processed

It is not. The generator returns before yielding anything, and the interface
shows the raw material with no interpretation under it.

This is not a placeholder or a failure path. A program that always answers is
worth less than one that sometimes refuses, and a reading that occasionally
gives you the raw find and no commentary is the cheapest way to keep that true.

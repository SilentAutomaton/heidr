The Fool|A step taken before the ground is checked.|Recklessness dressed as courage.
The Magician|The tools are already on the table.|Skill spent on the wrong thing.
The High Priestess|What is known but not yet said.|Secrecy that has stopped serving you.
The Empress|Something growing that needs only time.|Smothering what you meant to feed.
The Emperor|Structure that holds weight.|Rules kept after their reason left.
The Hierophant|The way it has always been done.|Doctrine repeated without belief.
The Lovers|A choice that decides who you are.|Avoiding the choice by making neither.
The Chariot|Two forces pulling in one direction at last.|Momentum with no hand on the reins.
Strength|The soft grip that holds the animal.|Force where patience was needed.
The Hermit|Withdrawal that is work, not hiding.|Isolation mistaken for depth.
Wheel of Fortune|The turn that owes you nothing.|Blaming the wheel for the ride.
Justice|The weighing, done properly.|A verdict reached before the evidence.
The Hanged Man|Seeing it from the other way up.|Waiting used as an excuse.
Death|The ending that makes room.|Clutching what is already gone.
Temperance|Two things mixed in the right measure.|Mixing everything until nothing tastes.
The Devil|The chain you keep because it is familiar.|The moment you notice it is not locked.
The Tower|The structure fails, suddenly, and correctly.|Rebuilding the same tower.
The Star|Quiet repair after the noise.|Hope rationed too carefully.
The Moon|Shapes in the dark that may be nothing.|Reading meaning into every shape.
The Sun|Plain daylight on a plain fact.|Cheerfulness worn as armour.
Judgement|The call you have been pretending not to hear.|Judging others to avoid the call.
The World|The circuit closed.|Finishing without noticing.
Ace of Wands|A spark worth acting on today.|A spark saved until it went out.
Two of Wands|Standing at the edge of the map.|Planning as a way of not leaving.
Three of Wands|The ships are out; wait properly.|Watching the horizon instead of working.
Four of Wands|A door reached and worth marking.|Celebration with nothing behind it.
Five of Wands|Friction that sharpens.|Fighting because fighting is easier.
Six of Wands|Recognised, deservedly.|Needing the applause to continue.
Seven of Wands|Holding the higher ground.|Defending what nobody is attacking.
Eight of Wands|Everything arriving at once.|Speed without aim.
Nine of Wands|Tired, still standing.|Guarding a wall long since taken.
Ten of Wands|Carrying more than one person should.|Refusing to put any of it down.
Page of Wands|Enthusiasm with no track record.|Announcing before starting.
Knight of Wands|Charging, and it works.|Charging, and it does not.
Queen of Wands|Warmth that others gather around.|Heat used to keep others out.
King of Wands|Vision someone else can follow.|Vision nobody else can see.
Ace of Cups|Feeling offered, unearned.|Feeling refused out of habit.
Two of Cups|A meeting of equals.|An imbalance both pretend not to see.
Three of Cups|Company that restores.|Company used to avoid quiet.
Four of Cups|The offered thing, unnoticed.|Boredom mistaken for wisdom.
Five of Cups|Grieving what spilled.|Two cups still standing behind you.
Six of Cups|The past, kindly.|The past, as a place to live.
Seven of Cups|Too many possibilities to choose.|Choosing the prettiest one.
Eight of Cups|Walking away from something adequate.|Leaving without admitting why.
Nine of Cups|Getting what you wanted.|Wanting it because you got it.
Ten of Cups|Enough, and shared.|A picture kept up for others.
Page of Cups|An odd, tender idea.|Sentiment used as evidence.
Knight of Cups|Following feeling somewhere real.|Following feeling anywhere at all.
Queen of Cups|Understanding without being asked.|Absorbing what is not yours.
King of Cups|Feeling under command.|Calm covering something unaddressed.
Ace of Swords|The clean cut of a clear thought.|Cleverness that wounds.
Two of Swords|Refusing to look at the choice.|The blindfold slipping.
Three of Swords|The hurt, named plainly.|Rehearsing the hurt.
Four of Swords|Rest that is medically necessary.|Rest refused until collapse.
Five of Swords|Won, at a cost you noticed later.|Winning as the only goal.
Six of Swords|Leaving toward something calmer.|Taking the same trouble along.
Seven of Swords|Taking the clever way out.|Being caught, deservedly.
Eight of Swords|Bound by what you believe about yourself.|The rope was loose the whole time.
Nine of Swords|The hours between three and five.|Fear rehearsed into fact.
Ten of Swords|The worst of it, over.|Insisting it is not over.
Page of Swords|Curiosity with sharp edges.|Questions asked to wound.
Knight of Swords|Fast, sure, and half right.|Fast, sure, and wrong.
Queen of Swords|Honesty that costs her something.|Coldness passed off as clarity.
King of Swords|Judgement earned by practice.|Judgement without warmth.
Ace of Pentacles|A solid thing offered.|A solid thing declined.
Two of Pentacles|Juggling, competently.|Juggling nothing worth holding.
Three of Pentacles|Craft recognised by other craftsmen.|Working alone on purpose.
Four of Pentacles|Holding on, sensibly.|Holding on past sense.
Five of Pentacles|Cold outside, light in the window.|Not going in.
Six of Pentacles|Giving and receiving in proportion.|Generosity with strings.
Seven of Pentacles|Waiting for what was planted.|Digging it up to check.
Eight of Pentacles|The tenth repetition, done well.|Repetition without attention.
Nine of Pentacles|Self-sufficiency, enjoyed.|Independence as a wall.
Ten of Pentacles|What outlasts you.|Wealth with nobody in it.
Page of Pentacles|Studying something useful.|Studying instead of doing.
Knight of Pentacles|Slow, thorough, unglamorous.|Slow past the point of use.
Queen of Pentacles|Practical care.|Care that keeps score.
King of Pentacles|Provision, steadily made.|Provision as the only measure.

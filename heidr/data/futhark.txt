# The twenty-four runes of the Elder Futhark, in their order.

# The names are reconstructions and belong to nobody. The glosses are ours:

Fehu|Wealth that moves. Hold it too tightly and it stops being wealth.
Uruz|Raw strength, with no opinion about where it goes.
Thurisaz|A thorn on the path. It was there before you were.
Ansuz|A word from above. Check who is really speaking.
Raidho|The road, and the wagon, and the wheel that is already loose.
Kenaz|A torch in a closed room. Now you can see the room.
Gebo|A gift, which is a debt nobody has named yet.
Wunjo|Joy, for as long as you do not ask how long.
Hagalaz|Hail. It falls on the just field and the other one.
Nauthiz|Need. The fire you make when there is nothing else to do.
Isa|Ice. Nothing moves, and you can walk across.
Jera|The harvest, which arrives when it arrives.
Eihwaz|The yew: green all winter, poison all the way through.
Perthro|The cup the lots are shaken in. Nobody has looked yet.
Algiz|An open hand held up. It stops things, and it asks for help.
Sowilo|The sun, which does not check whether you are ready.
Tiwaz|The god who gave up a hand to keep a promise.
Berkano|Birch: the first tree back after the fire.
Ehwaz|The horse. Two animals agreeing on a direction.
Mannaz|Other people, and what you owe them.
Laguz|Water. Deeper than it looks from the boat.
Ingwaz|The seed before it is anything.
Dagaz|Daybreak. The same field, now visible.
Othala|The inherited land, with everything buried in it.
